About Golden Pineapple Games & PunFiction

🍍 Who We Are: Golden Pineapple Games

PunFiction (punfiction.io) is the independent web game and interactive playground built by Golden Pineapple Games. Founded in 2026, our studio focuses on tight, curiosity-driven browser experiences—daily wordplay puzzles, statistical experiments, scientific simulations, and tactical sports games—that load instantly in any modern browser with zero downloads, zero subscriptions, and zero login walls.

Every project we publish pairs playable mechanics with transparent, deeply researched companion guides so curious players can explore the real geography, probability math, sensory biology, or sports strategy powering what happens on screen.

🎮 Our Lineup of Experiences & Guides

  • 1-Star Travel Reviews & Landmark Field Guides: A daily phonetic wordplay game where confused tourists leave scathing 1-star complaints about world landmarks they misread by a single syllable. Each monument is paired with a historical field guide, illustrated parody postcards, sarcastic owner responses, and a progressive 4-step hint system.
  • Word Vine & Botanical Sprint Guide: A fast-paced 40-node compound-word climb across three difficulty zones, reverse-compound loops, weather hazards, and Triple-Bloom growth spurts, with daily, weekly, and monthly global rankings.
  • The 8.3 Billionth & How the Math Works: An interactive population & information-theory observatory that starts with all 8,300,000,000 living humans on Earth and narrows the crowd across five powers-of-ten visual scales using overlap-dampened Bayesian conditioning and Shannon bits until statistically nobody else matches your trait combination.
  • UMWELT: Animal Optics & Sensory Biology Field Guide: A real-time WebGL2 multi-pass shader simulation spanning 3 ecosystems and 24 species vantage points—modeling ultraviolet reflectance, Snell’s Window refraction, non-spectral UV+Red tetrachromacy, lensless pinhole optics, 200 concave guanine-crystal mirrors, 8–12 μm thermal infrared pits, linear and circular polarization, 5 nV/cm shark electroreception, retinal biofluorescence filtering, silent binaural owl sound maps, dual-rod amphibian color, f/0.58 night scopes, chromatic defocus ranging, Cryptochrome-4 quantum magnetoreception, and 60–90 kHz ultrasonic sonar alongside 21 ecological field mysteries.
  • Flatball Tactics & Offense / Defense Playbooks: A 7v7 Ultimate Frisbee tactical simulator built around reading Vertical, Horizontal, Side ISO, Split, and Hexagon stacks, calling Man, Poach, Trap, Cup, Wall, and Box-and-1 counters, and adapting throws to live calm, breezy, and stiff headwind, tailwind, and crosswind physics.

🔬 How We Research & Craft Each Project

We deliberately avoid cookie-cutter templates and thin content. Each title on PunFiction starts from a real domain curiosity and goes through dedicated editorial and technical verification:

  • Hand-Curated Wordplay & Monument Research: Every 1-Star Travel Reviews clue is balanced across phonetic fairness, syllable rhythm, and progressive hint telemetry, while our consolidated Landmark Field Guides combine real architectural history, practical visitor advice, and custom parody illustrations.
  • Transparent Statistical Modeling: In The 8.3 Billionth, we publish our complete Plain-English and Mathematical Methodology—including Fréchet bounds, order-invariant Bayesian overlap dampening, demographic data tiers, and an interactive sandbox where readers can test the equations themselves.
  • Peer-Reviewed Optical Physics: The photoreceptor peak wavelengths, Malus's Law polarization controls, and ecological mysteries inside UMWELT: Animal Optics are grounded in published sensory-biology research, fully documented with citations in our Animal Optics Field Guide.
  • Lightweight, Standards-First Web Engineering: Everything runs on native HTML5, CSS3, Canvas/WebGL2, and vanilla JavaScript so pages stay fast, responsive, and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices without heavy frameworks or intrusive account requirements.
