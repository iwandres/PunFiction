Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Effective Date: November 27, 2026
Published By: Golden Pineapple Games (punfiction.io)

At PunFiction, operated by Golden Pineapple Games, we build daily wordplay games and interactive web experiences—including 1-Star Travel Reviews, Word Vine, The 8.3 Billionth, UMWELT: Animal Optics, and Flatball Tactics. We designed every experience so you can play immediately in your browser without creating an account or handing over personal contact details. This Privacy Policy explains what limited data is stored when you visit our site, how cookies and advertising partners operate, and how you can control or delete your data.

1. Local Browser Storage & Game Progress

When you solve a daily challenge in 1-Star Travel Reviews, record a daily climb in Word Vine, log discovered mysteries or survival missions in UMWELT: Animal Optics, or track Daily Cup matches in Flatball Tactics, your progress and display preferences are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. In The 8.3 Billionth, your active trait stack is kept temporarily in your tab's sessionStorage so you can read the math explainer and return without losing your progress.

If you choose to sync your streak across devices in 1-Star Travel Reviews using an optional PF-XXXXXX sync code, only that randomly generated anonymous code and your completed puzzle records are stored on our server—never your real name, email address, or physical identity. Similarly, optional climber handles entered in Word Vine leaderboards are user-chosen nicknames paired with an anonymous local token.

When you type an unscripted custom trait into The 8.3 Billionth, that single phrase is evaluated statelessly to estimate its worldwide prevalence. Inputs containing personal identifiers (such as full names, street addresses, phone numbers, emails, or social handles) are automatically rejected, and valid general trait estimates are cached anonymously by hashed normalized phrase without linking traits or trait combinations to any individual visitor.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on portions of PunFiction so that our daily puzzles, interactive simulations, and field guides remain free to read and play.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to PunFiction and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with consent options to manage ad personalization and storage preferences in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • For more details on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Analytics & Aggregate Puzzle Telemetry

We use Google Analytics and aggregate puzzle telemetry (such as how many players started Challenge #001 and how many hints were used on average) to calibrate puzzle difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, and Hard), measure page performance, and improve gameplay balance. All puzzle statistics displayed on the All User Stats board are aggregated across all players.

4. Newsletter Opt-In

If you voluntarily enter your email address into the optional update form on our homepage, your email is processed via MailerLite solely to send occasional announcements about new Golden Pineapple Games releases or major feature updates. We never sell or rent subscriber email lists, and you can unsubscribe with one click at any time.

5. Resetting or Deleting Your Data

You can clear your local puzzle history at any time using the Reset All Progress button inside the Profile & Device Settings menu in 1-Star Travel Reviews, or by clearing site data for punfiction.io in your web browser settings.

6. Contact & Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, cookies, or would like to request deletion of any voluntarily submitted newsletter or support information, please contact Golden Pineapple Games at goldenpineapplegames@gmail.com or visit our Contact Page.
