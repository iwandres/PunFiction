Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At PunFiction (punfiction.io), we build daily wordplay and geography puzzle games, including 1-Star Travel Reviews. We designed our games so you can play immediately in your browser without creating an account or handing over personal contact details. This Privacy Policy explains what limited data is stored when you visit our site, how cookies and advertising partners operate, and how you can control your preferences.

1. Local Browser Storage & Game Progress

When you solve a daily challenge, unlock a hint, or view your solved pins on the World Map, your progress is saved directly in your browser's localStorage. If you choose to sync your streak across devices using an optional PF-XXXXXX sync code, only that randomly generated anonymous code and the list of completed puzzle numbers are stored on our game server. We do not associate your puzzle progress with your name, email address, or physical identity.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on portions of PunFiction so that our daily puzzles and landmark guides remain free to read and play.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to PunFiction and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • For more details on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Analytics & Aggregate Puzzle Telemetry

We use Google Analytics and aggregate puzzle telemetry (such as how many players started Challenge #001 and how many hints were used on average) to calibrate puzzle difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, and Hard) and improve gameplay balance. All puzzle statistics displayed on the All User Stats board are aggregated across all players.

4. Newsletter Opt-In

If you voluntarily enter your email address into the optional update form on our homepage, your email is processed via MailerLite solely to send occasional announcements about new PunFiction games or major feature updates. You can unsubscribe with one click at any time.

5. Resetting or Deleting Your Data

You can clear your local puzzle history at any time using the Reset All Progress button inside the Profile & Device Settings menu in the game, or by clearing site data in your web browser.
