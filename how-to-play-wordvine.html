How to Play Word Vine 🌿

🌿 The Core Objective

In Word Vine, you grow a climbing bioluminescent vine up a 40-node daily botanical tower by chaining forward compound words before a relentlessly rising viewport camera leaves you in the dirt.

  • Binary Choice Controls: At every fork on the stem, two candidate words branch left and right. Press ← Left Arrow / A or Right Arrow / D → on desktop (or tap the Left / Right half of the screen on mobile) to pick the word that forms a valid forward compound or familiar phrase with the current stem word.
  • ✂️ Snipped (Lexical Strike): Choosing the decoy word immediately snaps your stem and ends the climb.
  • 🌑 Outrun (Viewport Starvation): The camera pans upward faster as time passes and as you complete more nodes. If you hesitate too long and the bottom edge of the screen overtakes your vine tip, your vine is starved of light!

🌸 How Forward Compound Chaining Works

Stem Word: PEANUT  |  Left Fork: BUTTER  |  Right Fork: SHELL (or a decoy)

Faint Node 3 Preview Ahead: 🌸 CUP

How to solve:
1. Look at the active stem word (PEANUT) and the faint preview floating above the fork (🌸 CUP).
2. Pick BUTTER to form PEANUT BUTTER, which then bridges directly into PEANUT BUTTER CUP on the third step!
3. Completing the 3rd word of a triplet triggers a 🌸 Triple-Bloom Growth Spurt, launching your vine upward with 1.5× impulse and awarding a +250 point bonus.

🌱 The 3 Botanical Climb Zones

Each daily vine is structured into three distinct botanical altitude zones, separated by a Graft Collar that briefly slows time (0.6 seconds of bullet-time) so you can adjust to the new linguistic rules:

  1. 🌱 Zone 1: The Roots (Nodes 1–12 — Linked 3-Part Triplets): Build four 3-part compound chains (3 steps each). A faint preview card above the active fork shows the 3rd word of the triplet ahead of time so you can spot the full three-word phrase. Even better, the final word of each triplet links forward into the next triplet (e.g., PEANUT → BUTTER → CUP 🔗 CUP → COFFEE → TABLE).
  2. 🪵 Zone 2: The Stem (Nodes 13–28 — 2-Part Double-Meaning Pivots): The training wheels come off! Every correct word you choose immediately becomes the stem for the next 2-part compound, pivoting meaning on the fly (e.g., COLD → SHOULDER → BLADE → RUNNER).
  3. ⚡ Zone 3: The Canopy (Nodes 29–40 — Terminal Velocity Sprints): High in the canopy, the camera approaches terminal velocity. Chain rapid-fire particle compounds and punchy two-word idioms (e.g., BACK → FIRE → WORK → BENCH) all the way to Node 40 to crown the canopy!

⚡ Physics, Altitude Lead & Scoring

Your score on each node rewards both climbing higher into the zones and staying comfortably ahead of the rising viewport camera:

  • Zone Base Points: Earn 100 pts per node in Zone 1, 200 pts per node in Zone 2, and 300 pts per node in Zone 3.
  • Altitude Lead Bonus: The higher your active vine tip sits above the bottom of the screen when you lock in a choice, the larger your Lead Bonus (up to +75 pts per node).
  • 🌸 Triple-Bloom Bonus: Completing the 3rd link of any Zone 1 triplet grants an instant +250 pts and a 1.5× upward growth spurt.
  • 120ms Input Buffer: Playing fast? You can queue your next Left/Right keystroke during the 180ms growth spurt animation so your rhythm never drops a beat.

🏆 Official Daily Runs, Practice & Leaderboards

Word Vine is designed for high-stakes daily competition with zero sign-in friction:

  • 🌿 First Attempt Counts: Your very first run each day is recorded as your Official Daily Climb on the Global Canopy Rankings and generates a spoiler-free Botanical Receipt you can copy and share with friends.
  • 🌱 Unlimited Practice Replays: Once your official daily score is locked, you can replay the daily vine as many times as you like in Practice Mode to explore missed compounds or test speedrun lines.
  • 👑 3-Tier Global Rankings: Compete across Daily (1st Run), Weekly (Best 5 of 7 days), and Monthly (Top 20 days) leaderboards!
