How to Play Word Vine 🌿

🌿 The Core Objective

In Word Vine, you grow a climbing bioluminescent vine up a 40-node daily botanical tower by chaining forward compound words before a relentlessly rising viewport camera leaves you in the dirt.

  • Binary Choice Controls: At every fork on the stem, two candidate words branch left and right. Press ← Left Arrow / A or Right Arrow / D → on desktop (or tap the Left / Right half of the screen on mobile) to pick the word that forms a valid forward compound or familiar phrase with the current stem word.
  • ✂️ Snipped (Lexical Strike): Choosing the decoy word immediately snaps your stem and ends the climb.
  • 🌑 Outrun (Viewport Starvation): The camera pans upward faster as time passes and as you complete more nodes. If you hesitate too long and the bottom edge of the screen overtakes your vine tip, your vine is starved of light!

🌸 How Forward Compound Chaining Works

Stem Word: PEANUT  |  Left Leaf: BUTTER  |  Right Leaf: SHELL (or a decoy)

Subtle Node Buds Below Stem: ● ── ◉ ── ○ (3-Node Link) or ● ── ◉ (2-Node Link)

How to solve:
1. Look at the active stem word (PEANUT) and check whether the subtle bud cluster shows a 2-Node Link (● ── ◉) or a 3-Node Link (● ── ◉ ── ○).
2. Pick BUTTER to form PEANUT BUTTER. Your chosen word immediately becomes the new stem word!
3. On a 3-Node Link, chaining the 3rd word (BUTTER → CUP for PEANUT BUTTER CUP) triggers a 🌸 Triple-Bloom Growth Spurt, launching your vine upward with 1.5× impulse and awarding a +250 point bonus.

🌱 The 3 Botanical Climb Zones

Each daily vine is structured into three distinct botanical altitude zones, separated by a Graft Collar that briefly slows time (0.6 seconds of bullet-time) so you can adjust to the new linguistic rules:

  1. 🌱 Zone 1: The Roots (Nodes 1–12 — 3-Node & 2-Node Linked Compounds): Build linked compound chains where each chosen word becomes the next stem word. Watch the subtle bud indicators for 3-part triplets that bloom on the 3rd word (e.g., PEANUT → BUTTER → CUP 🔗 CAKE → WALK → WAY).
  2. 🪵 Zone 2: The Stem (Nodes 13–28 — 2-Part Double-Meaning Pivots): The training wheels come off! Every correct word you choose immediately becomes the stem for the next 2-part compound, pivoting meaning on the fly (e.g., COLD → SHOULDER → BLADE → RUNNER).
  3. ⚡ Zone 3: The Canopy (Nodes 29–40 — Terminal Velocity Sprints): High in the canopy, the camera approaches terminal velocity. Chain rapid-fire particle compounds and punchy two-word idioms (e.g., BACK → FIRE → WORK → BENCH) all the way to Node 40 to crown the canopy!

🌦️ Special Twists: Reverse Loops, Speed Boosts & Weather

As your daily vine climbs higher into the canopy, dynamic botanical twists test both your vocabulary intuition and your reaction speed:

  • ⬅ Reverse Mode (Put Word in Front): When the vine curls into a ringed loop and displays the ⬅ PUT WORD IN FRONT banner, flip your mental reading direction—choose the leaf word that goes before the bottom stem word (for example, choosing PAN to go in front of CAKE to form PANCAKE).
  • ⚡ Speed Boost Rings: Passing through a golden ring launches a rapid-fire streak of brisk word pairs that reward quick, rhythmic left/right picks with huge extra altitude.
  • 🌬️ Wind Gusts: During windy stretches, candidate letters blow in and settle left-to-right across the leaves, rewarding sharp pattern recognition before the full word lands.
  • 🌧️ Rain Showers: Raindrops slide down the viewport glass and briefly distort letters as they pass—stay calm, track the stem context, and lock in your pick before the rising floor catches up.

⚡ Physics, Height & Scoring

Every climb is ranked first by Nodes Chained (out of 40) and second by total Vine Height (in meters):

  • Faster Picks Grow Taller Vines: Locking in valid compound links rapidly keeps your vine tip soaring above the rising camera floor and generates more vertical Height per node.
  • 🌸 Triple-Bloom Growth Spurts: Completing the 3rd link of a 3-node triplet sparks a Triple-Bloom boost that launches your vine upward with extra impulse.
  • 120ms Input Buffer: Playing at high tempo? You can queue your next Left/Right keystroke during the growth animation so your flow never stutters.

🏆 Daily Attempts & Global Canopy Rankings

Word Vine is built for daily replayability with zero login friction:

  • 🌿 3 Daily Climbs: Each daily theme gives you 3 official attempts to learn the canopy twists, dial in your speed, and post your best Nodes Chained and Height to the global leaderboard.
  • 🎯 Daily Top 5 Target Board: Right on the start card, you can see today's Top 5 climbers and the #1 height to beat before launching your run.
  • 👑 3-Tier Global Rankings: Compete across Daily, Weekly (#1 by Day), and Monthly (Top 20) leaderboards using any climber handle you choose!
