01 // INFORMATION THEORY

Why We Measure Progress in Shannon Bits

A linear progress bar fails immediately at planetary scale. Selecting a single common trait like “I play volleyball” (p = 0.12) eliminates 88% of humanity on click one—leaving the remaining 996,000,000 people squished into the final 12% of a linear bar for the rest of the session.

Instead, the Uniqueness Meter operates in Claude Shannon’s self-information (surprisal) space. Isolating a single individual from N0 = 8.3 × 109 equiprobable humans requires accumulating:

Hmax = log2(8.3 × 109) ≈ 32.95017 bits
Every 50/50 coin-flip trait contributes 1.00 bit; every 10× order-of-magnitude reduction contributes log2(10) ≈ 3.322 bits.

After stacking k active traits with effective conditional survival rates peff(1), …, peff(k), the unrounded expected headcount Ek and cumulative surprisal Ik satisfy:

Ek = N0 · ∏i=1..k peff(i),      Ik = ∑i=1..k −log2 peff(i) = log2(N0 / Ek)
02 // JOINT PROBABILITY & CORRELATION

Defeating Naive Independence: P(A ∩ B) ≠ P(A) · P(B)

If human traits were statistically independent Bernoulli variables, narrowing humanity would just multiply unconditional global prevalences pbase(i). In reality, human behaviors cluster around shared latent factors—geography, climate, fitness, income, sensory genetics, and subculture. By the chain rule of probability:

P(T1 ∩ T2 ∩ … ∩ Tk) = P(T1) · P(T2 | T1) · … · P(Tk | T1, …, Tk−1)

Whenever two traits have positive covariance—say, “I live above 5,000 ft” (1.1%) and “I backcountry ski” (0.045%)—the conditional probability P(T2 | T1) is substantially larger than the unconditional marginal P(T2). Treating them as independent would hallucinate uniqueness orders of magnitude too quickly.

Because we cannot store a dense 6,000 × 6,000 empirical covariance matrix—especially when users can type arbitrary free-text traits—our engine approximates the conditional chain through three hierarchical rules:

RULE A · P(A | B) = 1

Strict Subset & AI Custom Subsumption

Directed parent–child links (B ⊂ A) catch 100% inclusion across both curated and AI-estimated traits. Selecting Vegan alongside Vegetarian, Lives in Japan alongside Island resident, or typing “I own two Dachshunds” alongside Has a dog sets the broader parent to peff = 1.0 (0 bits).

RULE B · P(A ∩ B) = 0

Mutually Exclusive Partitions

Single-valued biological and demographic partitions (ABO/Rh blood type, country of residence, stature bracket, eye color, birth month, birth decade) enforce partition replacement rather than multiplying incompatible events.

RULE C · EXACT BAYES CONDITIONING

Bidirectional Country–Language & Geographic Coupling

Lifestyle tags alone miss sharp geographic couplings—for example, only 1.5% of Earth speaks Japanese, yet ~98.5% of residents in Japan do. Country tables store conditional rates r = P(L | C) for spoken languages, island geography, Southern Hemisphere, and high elevation, evaluated symmetrically in either selection order via Bayes’ theorem:

P(L | C) = r,      P(C | L) = min(0.995,  P(C) · r / P(L))

Result: clicking Lives in Japan → Speaks Japanese and Speaks Japanese → Lives in Japan converge to the exact same joint headcount (~121.0M humans) rather than hallucinating a 65× drop.

RULE D · PARTIAL OVERLAP (γ = 0.65)

Power-Law Cluster Dampening Across a Closed 36-Tag Latent Space

Every trait—curated, parametric, and Gemini-estimated—projects onto 1 to 3 tags in a closed 36-tag latent taxonomy 𝒞 (e.g., spicy_palate, culinary_diy, endurance_sport, coding_tech, polyglot). If active trait i shares at least one latent tag with mi prior active traits in your stack, its base prevalence is dampened via a diminishing-specificity exponent γ = 0.65:

peff(i) = (pbase(i))γmi,     where γ = 0.65

Why a power law in probability? Take −log2 of both sides and a neat identity pops out: Ieff(i) = γmi · Ibase(i). Power-law probability dampening is mathematically identical to geometric exponential decay in marginal Shannon information: the 1st trait in a cluster yields 100% of its bits, the 2nd yields 65%, the 3rd yields 42.25%, and the 4th yields 27.46%.

04 // DISCRETE STOPPING THRESHOLD & POISSON CLONES

The Ek ≤ 1.49 Threshold (and Does “1” Guarantee Zero Clones?)

Standard integer rounding maps 1.49 down to 1, while naive truncation or loose rounding would let someone at Ek = 1.9 claim victory early. Our engine enforces a strict piecewise boundary:

Ndisplay = 1  if Ek ≤ 1.49,    else max(2, round(Ek))

A subtle distinction for probability purists: Because you exist and conditioned the query on your own traits, you contribute a deterministic 1 to the global count. Among the remaining N0 − 1 strangers on Earth, the number of statistical clones Kothers follows a rare-event Binomial distribution well-approximated by Poisson(λ = Ek):

  • At Ek = 1.00 (32.95 bits): the expected number of strangers matching your list is λ = 1, giving P(Kothers = 0) = e−1 ≈ 36.8% chance of zero clones on Earth (and a 73.6% chance of at most one clone).
  • At Ek = 0.10 (36.27 bits): P(Kothers = 0) = e−0.1 ≈ 90.5% confidence that not a single other human alive shares your stack.
  • At Ek ≤ 0.01 (≥ 39.6 bits, our “Timeline Eraser” badge): P(Kothers = 0) ≥ 99.0%.
05 // BAYESIAN FERMI PRIORS & GUARDRAILS

How Custom Free-Text Traits Are Estimated Without Western Bias

When you type an unscripted trait into the omnibox (e.g., “I grow tropical pitcher plants indoors”), a structured-output LLM resolver performs a two-stage Fermi decomposition anchored to global UN demographic baselines—explicitly accounting for age distribution (infants through elderly) and global regional shares (Asia ~60%, Africa ~18%, Americas ~13%, Europe ~9%):

N̂trait = Nbase pool × P(quirk | base pool),      pbase = clamp(N̂trait / 8.3×109, 10−7, 0.95)
  • Hard Prevalence Floor (10−7 ≈ 830 humans): Custom traits are server-clamped at pbase ≥ 10−7 unless flagged as an established extreme medical or historical cohort (e.g., lightning-strike survivor at ~3 × 10−6) behind an explicit confirmation step.
  • Anti-Smuggling Compound Splits: Multi-clause inputs (“I’m a left-handed indie game dev who scuba dives”) are intercepted and split into atomic statements so each clause undergoes independent validation and γ = 0.65 tag-overlap checks.
  • Closed-Schema Tagging & Cache Flywheel: Custom estimates must emit 1–3 tags from the exact same 36-tag enum used by client-side curated traits, then persist by normalized SHA-256 hash so identical traits resolve globally with deterministic consistency.
06 // VISUALIZATION GEOMETRY

Rendering 8.3 Billion People on a 2D Canvas

Drawing 8.3 × 109 pixels is impossible on a screen with ~2 million pixels. The visualization uses a 5-tier Powers-of-Ten camera (106 → 103 → 1 → silhouettes → spotlight) keeping active particle counts between 1 and 9,999.

To pack thousands of dots into a circular field with uniform area density and zero grid Moiré artifacts, surviving particles n ∈ [0, N − 1] are arranged along Vogel’s golden-angle phyllotaxis spiral (the same packing geometry found in sunflower seed heads):

θn = n · π(3 − √5) ≈ n · 137.508°,      rn = Renv · √((n + 0.5) / N)
