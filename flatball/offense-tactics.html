Ultimate Frisbee Offense Tactics

In Ultimate Frisbee, you can’t run with the disc—so great offense is all about creating open space, reading how the defense lines up, and moving the disc before the 10-second stall count runs out. Here is how to read every defense and pick the winning offensive play!

1. Two Things to Check Before Every Throw

◀ Force Home vs. Force Away ▶ The defender guarding your thrower (the Marker) stands on one shoulder to block half the field. If they call Force Home (◀ Left), they are giving you the left side and blocking the right—unless you are already pinned right against the Home sideline (that’s a Trap!).
Man-to-Man vs. Zone Coverage In Man, every red defender shadows one of your cyan players. In Zone (like a 3-Man Cup or 2-3-2 Wall), defenders ignore your stack and guard spaces instead!
Vertical vs. Horizontal Stack Your team lines up in a Vert Stack (5 cutters in a center line to open both sidelines) or a Ho Stack (3 handlers back, 4 cutters across midfield) so teammates don’t crowd the same space.
💡 Pro Motion Trick Tap two different play cards before clicking Confirm! If the red Bot defenders follow your moving players, they’re in Man. If they stay parked in place, it’s Zone!

2. All 6 Offensive Plays Explained

Force-Side Cuts

TIMED CUTS

Take what the defense gives you! Instead of trying risky throws around the marker, your cutters take turns sprinting into the wide-open Force Side lane. One cutter sprints toward the thrower ("under") while the next sprints deep, keeping the lane clean.

🟢 Crushes: Force Open-Side Man (1-on-1 with tons of open grass) & Man + Reset Deny (you don’t even need a backward dump pass when downfield is wide open!). 🔴 Gets Stopped By: Sideline Trap Man (there is zero space between you and out-of-bounds) & 3-Man Cup Zone.

Break-Mark Swing

BREAK SIDE

Every downfield defender positions themselves assuming your thrower can’t throw past the marker. By faking high and stepping low ("breaking the mark") to swing the disc to the blocked side, every single defender is suddenly out of position!

🟢 Crushes: Sideline Trap Man! Breaking off the trapped sideline unlocks 30+ yards of wide-open field on the far side. 🔴 Gets Stopped By: 3-Man Cup Zone—trying to force a low pass straight through three cup defenders usually gets hand-blocked.

Popper & Wing Swing

BEAT CUP

A classic zone offense! The disc flies faster than defenders can run. Your handlers swing passes side-to-side to stretch the 3-person Cup, then zip a quick pass to a "Popper" flashing into the pocket behind the cup, who continues to an open sideline Wing.

🟢 Crushes: 3-Man Cup Zone (stretches the cup until gaps open right through the middle!). 🔴 Gets Stopped By: Man + Reset Deny (blitzing handler defenders jump wide lateral passes).

Give-and-Go Weave

SMALL BALL

Think basketball fast-break passing! As soon as a handler throws a short 8-yard pass, they don’t stand still—they immediately sprint upfield to get the disc right back. Quick triangles keep defenders on their heels.

🟢 Crushes: Man + Deep Poach! Because one defender is hiding deep in the end zone, you have a 6-on-5 advantage underneath for easy short passes all the way to a goal. 🔴 Gets Stopped By: Man + Reset Deny & 2-3-2 Wall Zone, which pack defenders into short lanes.

Flood & Over-the-Top

BEAT WALL

Zone defenders can only guard one depth at a time! By sending two receivers (`F1` short and `F2` deep) into the same side of the field and floating an overhead "hammer" or "blade" pass over the flat wall, one of your receivers is guaranteed to be open.

🟢 Crushes: 2-3-2 Wall Zone (arcs right over the flat 3-person midfield wall into the seam!). 🔴 Gets Stopped By: Force Open-Side Man (fast 1-on-1 defenders have time to run down high, floaty throws).

Deep Isolation Huck

LONG BOMB

A "huck" is Ultimate’s version of a Hail Mary or deep post route! Four teammates clear to the far sideline so your fastest cutter (`ISO`) gets half the field 1-on-1 to sprint down a 40-yard bomb into the end zone.

🟢 Crushes: Man + Reset Deny! When defenders gamble near the handler with zero deep safety help, punish them over the top! 🔴 Gets Stopped By: Man + Deep Poach (launching a huck at a waiting `SAG` safety is an easy interception!).
