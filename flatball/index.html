    🥏 How to Play Flatball Tactics

    Command a 7v7 Ultimate Frisbee squad (Cyan = YOU, Coral = BOT). You defend the Bottom End Zone (▼) and score in the Top End Zone (▲).

    1. Playing Defense (Force the Turn!)

    • Click PULL DISC to launch the point. Watch how the Bot’s 7 offensive players line up.
    • Preview & Confirm: Tap any strategy card to watch your players smoothly drift into that formation on the field. Test different options, then tap Confirm to run the play!
    • Ideal Counter: Locks down primary lanes and forces a Turnover (Block or Interception)!
    • Middle Strategy: Slows them down as they gain ~24 yards. Choose to Keep your defense or Switch!
    • Worst Strategy: Leaves a tactical opening that the Bot exploits for a Goal.

    2. Playing Offense (Force Home/Away & Man vs. Zone)

    • On a turn or pull, your team sets its stack (Vert or Ho) and the Bot Defense matches up to it!
    • Read Coverage: Did they follow your cutters 1-on-1 (Man, Deep Poach, Reset Blitz) or peel off into spaces (3-Man Cup, 2-3-2 Wall)?
    • Read Force (◀ HOME vs. AWAY ▶): Forcing toward the wide-open field invites Force-Side Cuts; forcing into a near sideline is a Trap — beat it with Break-Mark Swing!
    Pro Tip: Toggle Scout: PRO in the top bar anytime to hide formation labels and read pure 7v7 dot spacing like a club captain!
