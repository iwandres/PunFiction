    Flatball Tactics Playbook

    Coach a 7-on-7 Ultimate Frisbee team (Cyan = YOU, Coral = BOT). Defend your Bottom End Zone (▼) and score in the Top End Zone (▲)! First team to 3 points wins.

    30-Second Ultimate Lingo

    Handlers (H1, H2, H3) Your quarterbacks near the disc. They make crisp throws and stay ready for a backward "reset" pass if downfield is covered.
    Cutters (1–5) Your receivers downfield. They sprint toward the disc (under) or toward the end zone (deep) to get open.
    Stall Count (1 to 10) Once you catch the disc, you cannot run—you pivot on one foot! A defender counts to 10; if you don’t throw by 10, it’s a turnover.
    The Force (◀ Home / Away ▶) The defender on the thrower blocks one side of the field, forcing throws toward the other side (the Force Side).

    How Every Turn Works

    • Tap to Drift & Preview: Click any play card and watch the players smoothly glide into that formation on the field. Try different cards to compare how they line up!
    • Tap Confirm: Once you like the matchup on the field, click Confirm to launch the live play.
    • 3 Possible Outcomes: Calling the Ideal counter forces a Turnover on D (or scores a Goal on O!). Calling a Middle option lets the offense gain ~25 yards, where you can Keep your strategy or Switch. Calling the Worst option gives up a big play!
    Pre-Play Motion Trick: When you are on Offense, tap two different play cards before confirming. If the red Bot defenders follow your moving players, they are in Man-to-Man! If they stay parked in spaces, it’s a Zone!

    ⚡ Offense Tactics Guide (All 6 Plays Explained)

    Full Guide Page ↗

    When you get the disc, your team sets a stack (Vertical down the middle or Horizontal across the field) and the Bot Defense reacts. Check two things: Are they in Man or Zone? and Which sideline are they Forcing (◀ Home or Away ▶)?

    Force-Side Cuts

    TIMED CUTS

    Don’t fight the marker! Cutters take turns sprinting into the wide-open side the defense is giving you—one cuts in, and if covered, clears out so the next teammate has open grass.

    🟢 Best vs: Force Open-Side Man & Reset Deny (uses the big open lane downfield). 🔴 Watch out: Sideline Trap (no room!) or 3-Man Cup (blocked by the cup wall).

    Break-Mark Swing

    BREAK SIDE

    Your thrower uses a quick pivot fake to step around the marker and throw to the "blocked" side of the field—suddenly every defender is trailing on the wrong hip!

    🟢 Best vs: Sideline Trap Man (escapes the sideline trap into wide-open middle grass!). 🔴 Watch out: 3-Man Cup Zone (trying to throw through three cup defenders gets blocked).

    Popper & Wing Swing

    BEAT CUP

    Swing the disc side-to-side faster than the Cup can run! Once a gap opens, hit a "Popper" flashing right behind the cup wall and continue down the sideline wing.

    🟢 Best vs: 3-Man Cup Zone (pulls the three cup defenders apart!). 🔴 Watch out: Man + Reset Deny (tight handler defenders can jump wide lateral passes).

    Give-and-Go Weave

    SMALL BALL

    Just like basketball give-and-go! As soon as a handler passes the disc, they sprint forward for an instant return pass—marching downfield with quick, low-risk throws.

    🟢 Best vs: Man + Deep Poach (exploits the unguarded player underneath!). 🔴 Watch out: Man + Reset Deny & 2-3-2 Wall (crowds the short passing lanes).

    Flood & Over-the-Top

    BEAT WALL

    Send two receivers into the same zone area—one short, one deep—so a single zone defender can’t guard both, then float an arching pass right over the midfield wall!

    🟢 Best vs: 2-3-2 Wall Zone (beats the flat 3-person wall over the top!). 🔴 Watch out: Force Open-Side Man (1-on-1 defenders stay glued to high floaty throws).

    Deep Isolation Huck

    LONG BOMB

    Four teammates clear to the far sideline so your fastest sprinter gets a 1-on-1 runway—then your handler launches a 40-yard "huck" into the end zone!

    🟢 Best vs: Man + Reset Deny (punishes aggressive defenders with zero deep help!). 🔴 Watch out: Man + Deep Poach (throwing deep into a waiting safety = interception!).

    🛡️ Defensive Tactics Guide (All 6 Counters Explained)

    Full Guide Page ↗

    On Defense, look at how the 7 red Bot dots line up before picking your counter. Match your defense to take away their favorite throw!

    Vertical Stack: 5 cutters in a straight center line → watch out for deep shots!
    Horizontal Stack: 4 cutters spread wide across midfield → lots of space to run.
    Side Stack ISO: 4 cutters tucked on one sideline so 1 star cutter (`ISO`) gets the whole field!
    Handler Weave / Red Zone: Tight triangle near the disc or right on your goal line!

    Force Open-Side

    1v1 FORCE

    Solid, honest 1-on-1 defense. Every player guards one opponent, while your marker blocks throws to the break side so your teammates know exactly where cuts are coming from.

    🟢 Best vs: Balanced stacks when you want steady pressure without gambling. 🔴 Watch out: Side Stack ISO (leaving a star cutter 1-on-1 in open space is risky!).

    Hard Sideline Trap

    TRAP MAN

    Your marker blocks throws back to the middle of the field, pinning the offense against the sideline so the out-of-bounds line acts like an extra defender!

    🟢 Best vs: Red Zone End-Zone Sets (shrinks tight goal-line space until they panic). 🔴 Watch out: Horizontal Stack (if they break your mark, the whole middle is wide open!).

    Deep Poach / Sag

    HELP DEEP

    Six defenders guard 1-on-1 while your 7th defender (`SAG`) drops deep like a center fielder in baseball, ready to intercept any long throw toward your end zone!

    🟢 Best vs: Vertical Stack (takes away booming deep hucks down the open lanes!). 🔴 Watch out: Handler Weave & Red Zone (leaves an unguarded player open for short passes).

    Clam / Bracket

    BRACKET

    Teamwork coverage! Instead of chasing every fake, defenders sandwich the cutters—three guard short underneath passes (`U1–U3`) while two protect deep (`B1–B2`).

    🟢 Best vs: Side Stack ISO (double-teams the star cutter both short and deep!). 🔴 Watch out: Quick sideline-to-sideline handler swings before brackets can switch.

    Shutdown Reset

    NO DUMP

    Your second defender (`BLZ`) jumps right into the passing lane between the thrower and their backup handler, taking away their easy safety-valve reset pass!

    🟢 Best vs: Handler Weave (breaks their quick rhythm and drives the stall count to 9!). 🔴 Watch out: Vertical Stack (leaves single-covered cutters wide open for a deep shot!).

    3-Man Cup Zone

    3-3-1 ZONE

    Guard spaces instead of people! Three defenders (`C1–C3`) build a curved wall around the thrower to block forward throws and force risky, floaty passes.

    🟢 Best vs: Horizontal Stack (clogs wide cutting lanes and forces 15+ short throws). 🔴 Watch out: Side Stack ISO & patient handlers who lob passes over the cup!
