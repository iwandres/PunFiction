    Flatball Tactics Playbook

    Coach a 7-on-7 Ultimate Frisbee team (Cyan = YOU, Coral = BOT). Defend your Bottom End Zone (▼) and score in the Top End Zone (▲)! First team to 3 points wins.

    30-Second Ultimate Lingo

    Handlers (H1, H2, H3) Your quarterbacks near the disc. They make crisp throws and stay ready for a backward "reset" pass if downfield is covered.
    Cutters (1–5) Your receivers downfield. They sprint toward the disc (under) or toward the end zone (deep) to get open.
    Stall Count (1 to 10) Once you catch the disc, you cannot run—you pivot on one foot! A defender counts to 10; if you don’t throw by 10, it’s a turnover.
    The Force (◀ Home / Away ▶) The defender on the thrower blocks one side of the field, forcing throws toward the other side (the Force Side).

    🌬️ Wind Levels: Calm, Breezy & Stiff Wind

    Watch the Wind Badge above the field and the wind streaks blowing across the turf! Because an Ultimate disc weighs only 175 grams, wind changes which plays work best:

    🍃 Calm (0 mph) Standard conditions! Every throw—short darts, upside-down hammers, and 40-yard hucks—flies true.
    🌬️ Breezy (9–12 mph) Noticeable drift on high passes. Avoid upside-down Overhead Hammers into a breezy headwind!
    💨 Stiff Headwind (18–22 mph) High throws pop up and blow backward! On O, stick to low cuts (Upline Strike, Popper Swing). On D, throw a 3-Person Cup!
    💨 Stiff Tailwind / Crosswind With a tailwind at your back, Deep ISO Hucks fly 50 yards! On D against a tailwind, drop a Deep Poach safety!

    How Every Turn Works

    • Tap to Drift & Preview: Click any play card and watch the players smoothly glide into that formation on the field. Try different cards to compare how they line up!
    • Tap Confirm: Once you like the matchup on the field, click Confirm to launch the live play.
    • 3 Possible Outcomes: Calling the Ideal counter forces a Turnover on D (or scores a Goal on O!). Calling a Middle option lets the offense gain ~25 yards, where you can Keep your strategy or Switch. Calling the Worst option gives up a big play!
    Pre-Play Motion Trick: When you are on Offense, tap two different play cards before confirming. If the red Bot defenders follow your moving players, they are in Man-to-Man! If they stay parked in spaces, it’s a Zone!

    ⚡ Offense Tactics Guide (All 9 Plays Explained)

    Full Guide Page ↗

    Each turn deals 4 plays from our 9-play Ultimate playbook. Check three things before confirming: Man vs. Zone, The Force (◀ Home, Away ▶, or Middle), and The Wind!

    Force-Side Cuts

    OPEN LANE

    Don’t fight the marker! Cutters take turns sprinting into the wide-open side the defense is giving you—one cuts in, and if covered, clears out so the next teammate has open grass.

    🟢 Best vs: 1v1 Man-to-Man with an open force lane. 🔴 Watch out: Sideline Trap, Force Middle, or 2-3-2 Wall Zone.

    Break-Mark Swing

    BREAK SIDE

    Your thrower uses a quick pivot fake to step around the marker and throw to the "blocked" side of the field—suddenly every defender is trailing on the wrong hip!

    🟢 Best vs: Sideline Trap Man (escapes the boundary trap into wide-open grass!). 🔴 Watch out: 3-Man Cup Zone (trying to throw through three cup defenders gets blocked).

    Upline Strike Cut

    POWER CUT

    Your backup handler fakes backward, then explodes upfield past a face-guarding defender into "Power Position"—catching the disc in full stride facing the end zone!

    🟢 Best vs: Man + Reset Deny (Dump Blitz) & Tight Press in a Stiff Headwind! 🔴 Watch out: 3-Man Cup Zone (three Cup defenders wall off the upline lane!).

    Dump-Swing Reversal

    SWING WIDE

    Drop a safe pass slightly backward ("dump") to your center handler, who immediately whips a wide "swing" pass all the way to the far sideline before the defense can rotate!

    🟢 Best vs: Force Middle Man (outflanks the crowded middle!). 🔴 Watch out: Man + Reset Deny (`BLZ`) & Box-and-1 (`CH1` jumps the dump pass!).

    Give-and-Go Weave

    SMALL BALL

    Just like basketball give-and-go! As soon as a handler passes the disc, they sprint forward for an instant return pass—marching downfield with quick, wind-proof throws.

    🟢 Best vs: Man + Deep Poach (exploits 6-on-5 space underneath!). 🔴 Watch out: Tight Under Press & Man + Reset Deny (jumps short passes!).

    Popper & Wing Swing

    CUP BEATER

    Swing the disc side-to-side faster than the Cup can run! Once a gap opens, hit a "Popper" flashing right behind the cup wall and continue down the sideline wing.

    🟢 Best vs: 3-Man Cup Zone—plus 2-3-2 Wall & Box-and-1 in a Stiff Headwind! 🔴 Watch out: Tight Under Press (fronting defenders jump short swings!).

    Flood & Over-Top

    WALL BEATER

    Send two receivers into the same zone wing—one short, one deep—so a single zone defender can’t guard both, then float a pass over the midfield wall!

    🟢 Best vs: 2-3-2 Wall Zone (overloads one wing at two depths!). 🔴 Watch out: 1v1 Man-to-Man (crowds extra defenders into one lane!) & Stiff Headwinds!

    Overhead Hammer

    OVERHEAD

    Thrown overhead like a baseball pitch so the disc flies upside-down in a high arc, dropping right over a zone box into an uncovered receiver’s hands!

    🟢 Best vs: Box-and-1 Junk (arcs right over the 4-defender Box!). 🔴 Watch out: Man + Deep Poach (`SAG` tracks high throws) & Breezy/Stiff Headwinds!

    Deep ISO Huck

    LONG BOMB

    Four teammates clear to the far sideline so your fastest sprinter gets a 1-on-1 runway—then your handler launches a 40-yard "huck" into the end zone!

    🟢 Best vs: Tight Under Press (no deep help!) & 1v1 Man in a Stiff Tailwind! 🔴 Watch out: Man + Deep Poach, 3-Man Cup, or Stiff Headwinds!

    🛡️ Defensive Tactics Guide (All 9 Counters Explained)

    Full Guide Page ↗

    On Defense, spot which of the 9 Bot Formations they lined up in—and check the Wind Badge—to call the shutdown counter!

    Vertical / Horizontal Stack: Center line (→ Deep Poach) or 4-wide spread (→ 3-Person Cup).
    Side ISO / Split Stack: 1 isolated star (→ Clam Bracket) or twin sideline stacks (→ Force Middle).
    Hexagon Spread / Star Playmaker: Triangles with a hub (→ 2-3-2 Wall) or `ACE` QB (→ Box-and-1).
    Popper-Wing Spread: 3-2-2 Zone-Buster (→ Force Open-Side 1v1!).
    Handler Weave / Red Zone: Handler triangle (→ Shutdown Reset) or goal-line set (→ Sideline Trap).

    Force Open-Side

    1v1 FORCE

    Solid, honest 1-on-1 defense. Every player guards one opponent while your marker blocks throws to the break side so teammates know where cuts are coming from.

    🟢 Best vs: Popper-Wing Spread (locks down Zone-Buster 1v1!) & Handler Weave in a Stiff Tailwind! 🔴 Watch out: Side Stack ISO (leaving a star cutter 1-on-1 in huge space!).

    Sideline Trap

    TRAP MAN

    Your marker blocks throws back to the middle, pinning the offense against the boundary so the out-of-bounds line acts like an extra defender!

    🟢 Best vs: Red Zone Sets & stacks pinned on the downwind sideline in a Stiff Crosswind! 🔴 Watch out: Horizontal Stack (3 spread handlers easily break the trap).

    Force Middle

    FORCE MID

    Instead of picking one sideline, your marker always forces throws back toward the center of the field—right where your `MID` help defender is waiting!

    🟢 Best vs: Split Stack (shuts off sideline hucks on both sides!). 🔴 Watch out: Hexagon Spread (feeds the disc straight into their center HUB!).

    Deep Poach

    HELP DEEP

    Six defenders guard 1-on-1 while your 7th defender (`SAG`) drops deep like a center fielder in baseball to sky long throws toward your end zone!

    🟢 Best vs: Vertical Stack—plus Horizontal & Split Stacks when Bot has a Stiff Tailwind! 🔴 Watch out: Handler Weave, Red Zone, Split Stack (calm), or Stiff Headwinds!

    Clam Bracket

    BRACKET

    Teamwork coverage! Defenders sandwich cutters from both sides—three guard short underneath passes (`U1–U3`) while two protect deep (`B1–B2`).

    🟢 Best vs: Side Stack ISO (double-teams the isolated star short and deep!). 🔴 Watch out: Handler Weave (leaves 4 handlers playing 4-on-2 in the backfield!).

    Shutdown Reset

    NO DUMP

    Your `BLZ` defender jumps right into the lane between the thrower and their backup handler, taking away their easy safety-valve reset pass!

    🟢 Best vs: Handler Weave (breaks small-ball rhythm and drives stall to 9!). 🔴 Watch out: Vertical Stack or when Bot has a Stiff Tailwind!

    3-Person Cup

    3-3-1 CUP

    Three defenders (`C1–C3`) form a curved wall around the thrower to block forward lanes and force 15+ short passes. Ultimate’s #1 wind weapon!

    🟢 Best vs: Horizontal Stack—plus Vertical, Side ISO, & Split Stacks in a Stiff Headwind! 🔴 Watch out: Popper-Wing Spread (their 3-2-2 Zone-Buster shreds the Cup pocket!).

    2-3-2 Wall Zone

    WALL ZONE

    Two front chasers hassle handlers while a 3-player flat Wall (`W1–W3`) parks across midfield to intercept medium diagonal passes!

    🟢 Best vs: Hexagon Spread (sits right in their diagonal passing triangles!). 🔴 Watch out: Star Playmaker Hub & Popper-Wing Spread (dissects stationary zones!).

    Box-and-1 Junk

    BOX + 1

    Borrowed straight from basketball! One lockdown chaser (`CH1`) glues 1-on-1 onto their best player while four teammates guard a midfield Zone Box (`B1–B4`).

    🟢 Best vs: Star Playmaker Hub (erases their `ACE` quarterback!). 🔴 Watch out: Split Stack (central Box guards empty grass while twin sidelines run free!).
